Your device, seeing with sound

The speaker sends out a tone too high for you to hear, and the microphone listens to its echoes. Anything that moves changes the echo: your hand in the air, someone walking in, your chest as you breathe. Turn the volume up (no headphones), give the microphone permission, and keep still for about ten seconds while it finds the best inaudible tone for your speaker.

Breathing, from a distance

Put the device on a table 30 to 60 cm from your chest, screen toward you, and sit still. Every breath moves your chest a few millimetres, which turns the echo by a measurable amount. Give it about 30 seconds.

Is anyone moving?

Leave the device in a room. It hears movement anywhere in front of it, a few metres away, with no camera: the timeline shows the last two minutes.
